Ta-Done! privacy

Ta-Done! is a private planner used only by Dorothy Ang. Only her Google account can sign in.

When she signs in, Ta-Done! uses Google Calendar, Google Tasks and Google Drive (only files Ta-Done! creates) to show her schedule, keep her tasks in step, and save her journal, time log and backups to her own Drive.

Her Ta-Done! data is stored privately on this website's hosting (Cloudflare) and in her own Google account. It is never sold, shared, or used for anything else. Signing out or removing access in her Google account settings stops all access.
