From Davao de Oro, Philippines

Dear future client,

Thank you for taking the time to read this. There's more to me than what you'll find on my CV, and I'd love for you to get to know that side of me.

When I was a little girl, I always wanted to be my grandfather's secretary. Other kids dreamed of being doctors or lawyers, but I wanted to be the one keeping his things in order, reminding him what was next and making his day a little easier. I didn't know it then, but that feeling never left me. Doing things for others has always come naturally to me. It is simply who I am.

For the past few years, I have been running my own businesses and working in our family business. I learned more than I ever expected: how to lead a team, handle suppliers and finances, solve problems on the spot and keep everything moving when things get busy.

Working with family taught me patience, honest communication and how to care for relationships and responsibilities at the same time. It also helped me see myself more clearly. I realized I thrive behind the scenes, making things easier for someone else. I don't need to be the boss. What fills my heart is seeing the people I work with do well.

This is the first time in a long time that I am putting myself out there, and I will be honest: it takes a little courage. But I am excited. Along the way, I learned how much balance matters, and taking care of my mental health helps me show up focused, calm and ready every day.

More than anything, I want to help you grow. I want to be there for the journey, through the busy seasons and the quiet ones, the big wins and the hard days. I would love for us to grow together.

I know I have been away from the workforce for some time. I am eager to learn, quick to adapt and I take my work seriously. I will learn your systems, your preferences and your way of doing things, and I will keep learning and growing with you.

I hope you will take a chance on me. If you do, I promise to give it my whole heart.

With warmth,

Dorothy

How I work

Nice to meet you!

I know how it feels to have too much on your plate. When I was running my own businesses, I was the one answering every message, following up on every order and finishing the admin long after everyone else had gone home. Back then, I really wished I had someone I could trust to take some of it off my hands. Now I get to be that person for someone else.

My goal is simple. I want to be the person you can truly count on, the one who quietly takes care of the details and carries some of the weight with you.

I've been in your shoes. I use that experience to think for you and always with you.

  • I think ahead

    I keep an eye out for risks before they turn into problems: a supplier running late, a busy season coming up, a customer getting frustrated. I'll flag it early and come to you with a plan.

  • I think for you, not around you

    I'll do the legwork and bring you options with my recommendation. All you need to do is decide. The final call is always yours, and I'll never go over your head.

  • I look out for your bottom line

    I'll look for cheaper shipping, a repair instead of a replacement or a better deal from a supplier. Every peso and every dollar counts.

  • I make repetitive work easier

    When a task comes up again and again, I look for simple ways to save time such as a template, a macro or an automation, and I add it to your SOPs.

  • I check the numbers

    I'll look at the data regularly and tell you honestly what I see: good or bad.

I believe in integrity at work. I'm completely open to you monitoring my work with Time Doctor or any time-tracking software you prefer.

I know a lot of people don't like being tracked. Honestly, I don't mind at all. It keeps me accountable, and it lets you see my work without having to check in on me all the time. It's completely up to you.

So when I set up my office, I gave it its own room, separate from my bedroom, and placed my desk in the command position: facing the door, with a solid wall behind me and a window view. It helps me stay focused during work hours and switch off when the day is done.
